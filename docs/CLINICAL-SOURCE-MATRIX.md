## Recorded source-check scope

- **fogsi-hdp-gcpr-2026**: PDF served from fogsi.org (2026 upload path) — definitions, HDP-Gestosis score, treatment threshold ≥140/90 (WOG), targets ≤140/90, delivery timing (GH term; mild PE 37 wks; severe PE after 34 wks; eclampsia deliver once stabilised post-MgSO4; ≤24 wks immediate delivery better; individualise 24–33+6)
- **isshp-2021**: Full PDF: standardised BP measurement; proteinuria PrCr ≥30 mg/mmol, ACR ≥8 mg/mmol or ≥300 mg/day or dipstick 2+ if confirmatory testing unavailable; no 'mild/severe' PE label in ongoing pregnancy; treat all HDP with antihypertensives; severe HTN ≥160/110 treat within minutes-hours monitored; target DBP 85; MgSO4 for eclampsia + PE with severe HTN/neuro features; aspirin ≥150 mg night from <16 to 36 wks after multivariable screening; calcium <900 mg/d intake → supplement ≥500 mg/d; exercise recommended; LMWH/vitC/E/folate NOT for PE prevention
- **acog-chap-advisory-2022**: Publisher summary + independent 2025 review confirming advisory remains operative: initiate/titrate therapy at ≥140/90 mmHg for chronic HTN (treatment threshold, not target). Note ACOG PB222 base document last reaffirmed — verify current reaffirmation date when next audited.
- **who-anc-2016-updates**: WHO pages + NCBI compendium: daily IFA 30–60 mg elemental iron + 400 µg folic acid; intermittent IFA only where daily not acceptable & anaemia prevalence <20%; anaemia in ANC → 120 mg elemental iron daily until Hb normal; MMS recommended only within rigorous research contexts (supersedes 2016); ≥8 contacts
- **who-pph-consolidated-2025**: WHO page + NCBI full recommendations chapter + Bleeding-after-birth provider guide: PPH definition >500 mL vaginal / >1000 mL CS or any loss compromising haemodynamics; TXA 1 g IV over 10 min as early as possible within 3 h of birth, repeat 1 g if bleeding continues at 30 min or restarts within 24 h; oxytocin 10 IU in 100–500 mL IV as fast as possible within bundle; TXA-bupivacaine storage separation warning; heat-stable carbetocin context where cold chain unavailable
